Scope of Service

Complete UAE VAT Compliance Solutions

Meticulously studying business transactions to ensure full compliance with UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) regulations and prevent administrative penalties.

Pillar 01

Supply Classification

Comprehensive review of commercial revenue to categorize transactions across taxable standard rate (5%), zero-rated (0%), exempt supplies, intra-GCC supplies, and out-of-scope transactions.

Pillar 02

Purchases & Import Analysis

Detailed analysis of local supplier invoices, direct foreign imports through UAE customs, reverse charge mechanism (RCM) calculations, and intra-GCC purchases documentation.

Pillar 03

Input Tax Recovery (ITC)

Rigorous assessment of commercial invoices to maximize legitimate claimable input VAT while segregating blocked expenses (entertainment, personal motor vehicles, non-business costs).

Pillar 04

FTA Regulatory Advice

Expert ongoing advice for full compliance with UAE VAT decrees, documentation hygiene, proper tax invoicing formats, and mandatory statutory records retention.

Pillar 05

Return Filing & Payments

Timely compilation, mathematical reconciliation, and electronic submission of periodic VAT returns via the official FTA portal, accompanied by clear payment tracking.

Pillar 06

Penalty Prevention & Audit Defense

Proactive pre-submission audits to identify errors, eliminate compliance exposures, and defend your business filings during Federal Tax Authority verification queries.
