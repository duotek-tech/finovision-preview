Specialized Tax Practice

VAT / Tax Consultancy

We meticulously study our clients’ business transactions and guide them to align with UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) tax laws and regulations. We assist our clients to maintain proper documents and records and submit tax returns to ensure full compliance with the regulations and prevent administrative penalties.

Led by certified holders of the Diploma in GCC VAT Compliance (PwC / ATT), we handle supply classification, local and import Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM), claimable versus blocked input VAT (ITC), and return submissions.
